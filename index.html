DIRECTIVE // 001

The Bureau of Cipher and Leaders is a private community where members can communicate, collaborate, compete, and build things together.

Members choose how they are represented inside the Bureau. Leaders maintain the member directory so the community can stay organized and welcoming.

The point isn't secrecy for the sake of secrecy. The atmosphere is simply different here. Less noise. More curiosity.
